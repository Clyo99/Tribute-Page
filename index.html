Scott Cawthon

Summary

Scott Cawthon was born on June 4th of 1978 in Houston, Texas, U.S., starting his video game designer career at (presumely) 1996 developing family-friendly Christian video games to little success. He eventually went to making horror games due to a myriad of circumstances, creating the notable Five Nights at Freddy's game franchise. Currently as of today, he is retired and spending time with his family.

Timeline

      Scott Cawthon atteends the Art Institute of Houston
      He makes his first professional game which is called Iffermoon.
      He also makes stuff like The Pilgrim's Progress: The Video Game (2011) and The Desolate Hope (2012).
      In 2013, Scott makes Chipper & Sons Lumber Co., which was the game that caused Scott to make Fnaf after seeing the mass criticism for Chipper & Sons Lumber Co..
      In 2014, Scott releases Five Night's at Freddy's, and then, after it gained popularity and was well recieved by critics, Five Nights at Freddy's 2 was made.
      In 2015, Fnaf 3 was made and released, with this being the "planned" finale of the series, but we ALL know that happened to not be the case.
      Also in 2015, Fnaf 4 was made and released alongside a free Halloween update.
      Then there's Fnaf World released in 2016, and it didn't have a good time as reviews were mixed due to glitches and other issues, even had to pulled off Steam after 4 days, and a altered version was put in Gamejolt for free sometime after.
      Also in 2016, Fnaf: Sister Location, or Fnaf 5, was released.
      In 2017, Fnaf Pizzeria Simulator was released and its ending felt like a era had ended and another one had joined.
      2018 was when Scott made the seventh installment of the series, Ultimate Custom Night, for free on Steam.
      Scott Cawthon partners up with Steel Wool Studios, which then resulted in Fnaf: Help Wanted to be made released, alongside a downloadable content update for Halloween.
      Scott makes the Fazbear Fanverse Intiative to support Fnaf games made by fans.
      On June 16th, 2021, Scott goes into retirement after some controversy about him donating money to Replublican Party politicians. Though this didn't hurt his reputation at all and he's glad he is able to spend more time with his kids. He intended to appoint a successor to ensure that the Five Nights at Freddy's franchise would continue, while he himself would continue to occupy a lesser role in its development. As of 2026, Cawthon has overarching creative control over the franchise and the exclusive rights over it.
